### **Establish the business context
Chat Mode - Agent**
💡

We are beginning a fresh Agentic SDLC workflow in the current repository.

The business problem is:

Smart Fare Alert.

A user should be able to receive an alert when the fare for a relevant bus journey meets a condition that matters to the user.

At this stage, do NOT design or implement the feature.

Create a business-context document that captures only what is currently known.

Include:

1. Problem statement
2. Business objective
3. User/business value
4. High-level capability
5. Known actors
6. Known inputs
7. Known outputs
8. Known constraints
9. Explicit assumptions
10. Open questions
11. Decisions that still require human/business clarification

Important rules:

- Do not invent APIs.
- Do not invent database schemas.
- Do not invent notification providers.
- Do not invent pricing algorithms.
- Do not invent SLAs.
- Do not invent architecture.
- Do not invent detailed acceptance criteria.
- Do not implement anything.

Clearly label assumptions and open questions.

Use the existing repository context from AGENTS.md where relevant, but do not treat technical possibilities as confirmed business requirements.

Create the document only. Do not modify application code.
