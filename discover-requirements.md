Your task is REQUIREMENTS DISCOVERY ONLY.

Do not implement anything.
Do not modify any files.

Analyze the Smart Fare Alert business context and produce a requirements-discovery report.

The report must contain:

1. Confirmed business requirements
2. Functional requirements that can be derived directly from the business context
3. User goals
4. Business goals
5. Actors
6. Inputs
7. Outputs
8. Business rules explicitly stated in the context
9. Assumptions
10. Open questions
11. Missing information
12. Requirements that require business approval
13. Requirements that require technical investigation

For every requirement, clearly indicate whether it is:

- Confirmed
- Derived
- Assumption
- Open question

Do not turn assumptions into requirements.

Do not invent:

- APIs
- database schemas
- notification providers
- pricing algorithms
- infrastructure
- SLAs
- performance numbers
- security mechanisms
- architecture decisions

The existing repository may help identify current capabilities, but repository capabilities must not automatically become Smart Fare Alert requirements.

At the end, provide a "Requirements Readiness Assessment" explaining whether the information is sufficient to proceed to detailed engineering design and what decisions are still blocking.
